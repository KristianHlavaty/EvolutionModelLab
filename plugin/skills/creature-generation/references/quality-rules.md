# Image quality rules

Apply these rules to generation and review instructions without claiming semantic validation:

- Preserve immutable manifest features and the current locked identity.
- Treat preferred features as guidance and forbidden features as hard exclusions.
- Incorporate stored feedback in its recorded priority; do not add unapproved anatomy or biology.
- Keep camera, facing, canvas dimensions, anchor, style, lighting, palette, material, and transparency requirements explicit.
- Produce real PNG outputs. Keep originals untouched; Model Lab creates thumbnails, crops, sheets, and exports separately.
- For concepts/refinements, ask for ten controlled candidates by default and forbid animation.
- For canonical references, generate one requested view per image and preserve identity.
- For animations, establish key poses before intermediates and keep scale/anchor/identity stable across frames.
- Repair one defective frame with its neighbors as continuity context.
- Treat size, alpha, bounds, duplicate, and continuity checks as mechanical evidence only. Require human visual review for anatomy, identity, palette, and motion quality.
- Never convert a warning into silent rejection or a visual guess into approval.
